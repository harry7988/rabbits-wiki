# ⛔ Foods Rabbits Must Never Eat

<Danger title="If your rabbit ate something on this list">

Rabbits **physically cannot vomit** — inducing vomiting is both useless and dangerous (aspiration). After any ingestion:

1. Remove the remaining food; **note what / how much / when**
2. Do **not** induce vomiting, give milk, or give activated charcoal on your own
3. Contact a rabbit-savvy vet — Oxbow's guidance: **after ingesting any item below, contact a vet regardless of amount**<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite>

Full poisoning protocol → [中文：中毒与误食处置](../../emergencies/poisoning)

</Danger>

## Toxic (potentially fatal)

| Food | Toxin / effect |
|---|---|
| **Chocolate / cocoa** | Theobromine + caffeine; darker = more toxic; can be fatal<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Avocado** | Persin — respiratory distress, heart failure<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Onion / garlic / leeks / chives** (raw or cooked) | Hemolytic anemia — red-cell destruction<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Fruit seeds & pits** — apple, pear, peach, cherry, plum | Cyanogenic glycosides; **flesh is fine, seeds/pits removed**<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Raw potato** (and sprouts) | Solanine + high starch<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Rhubarb** | High oxalate, disrupts calcium metabolism<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Mushrooms** | Mycotoxin risk, raw or cooked<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Fava / kidney beans** | High starch → diarrhea, bloat, dysbiosis<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |

## Should never be fed

| Food | Why |
|---|---|
| **Meat / eggs / dairy** (incl. yogurt drops) | Rabbits are strict herbivores; animal protein destroys cecal flora; lactose-intolerant<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Bread / crackers / chips / pasta** | High starch and sugar → dysbiosis, obesity, potentially fatal enterotoxemia<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Nuts & seeds** | Excessively fatty; shell fragments risk aspiration |
| **Iceberg lettuce** | Contains lactucarium, nearly nutrient-free; harmful in quantity<cite><a href="#ref1">[1]</a></cite> |
| **Sugary drinks / human snacks** | Never |
| **Cat or dog food** | Protein/fat formulas wholly unsuitable |

## Common misconceptions

- **"Rabbits love carrots"** → carrots are a **sugary treat**, not a staple (cartoon myth); feed as a vegetable in small amounts
- **Cat hairball gel** → harmful for rabbits (oil coats the mass, makes it water-resistant) — [the hairball truth](../../supplies/hairball-remedy) (Chinese)
- **"A little yogurt aids digestion"** → dairy is contraindicated; see the [probiotics topic](../../supplies/probiotics) (Chinese)
- **Self-dosing vitamins** → fat-soluble vitamins accumulate to toxicity; do not supplement without a vet

## Houseplants & outdoor plants

Many common plants are toxic (lily, azalea, pothos, ivy, tulip bulbs…). Use our [toxic plant checker](../../tools/) (Chinese UI, species names searchable) — every entry carries a Cornell/HRS source.

Outdoors: grass treated with pesticides is off-limits; **lawnmower clippings ferment and cause bloat** — never feed.
